###  NCAP Score Calculation

It is possible to switch the calculation from Area based (GTR) to NCAP score based by choosing the option from the Regulation drop down found on the Calculation Tab.

![](../Storage/primer-22-1/ncap-score-calculation/ncap-score-calculation-2024-04-09.png)

There are 3 NCAP options:

* EuroNCAP v9
* C-NCAP 2021
* EuroNCAP v8 (OLD)

When in NCAP mode the tool will calculate the NCAP score based on the protocol and present the data using NCAP colouring:

![](../Storage/primer-22-1/primer_links/sect_6/pedestrian_markup/hic_area_06.bmp)

The scores are presented in the table:

![](../Storage/primer-22-1/primer_links/sect_6/pedestrian_markup/hic_area_table2.bmp)
