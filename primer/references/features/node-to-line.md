**Node to Line**

Calculates the shortest distance and the normal vector from the line defined by nodes N1 and N2 to node N3.

Nodes/geometry points may be screen-picked, have their labels typed in, or selected using the standard popup options. The nodes need not be in the same model.

![](../Storage/primer-22-1/fig_node_to_line.png)

The projected distance on the XY, YZ and ZX coordinate planes is also calculated.
