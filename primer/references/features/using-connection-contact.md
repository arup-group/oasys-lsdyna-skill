####  Using Connection &gt; Contact

This function will report a summary of errors in hover text activated when the mouse is over the dark grey button.

![](../Storage/primer-22-1/primer_links/sect_6/connection/contact_3.gif)

Tied and untied connections may be sketched with Sketch Conx and contacts used for connections may be accessed directly by Edit Contact . Problematic connections may easily be put on the table using Error-&gt;table , where the show conx & panels feature will enable you to display the area of interest. Delete may be used to remove contacts selected on the object menu.
