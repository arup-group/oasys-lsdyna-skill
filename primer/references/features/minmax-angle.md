####  Min-Max Angle

The internal angle of each vertex of each face, within an element, is calculated, with the largest and smallest then being compared to the maximum and minimum internal angle criteria respectively

Checking the internal angles of triangular and quadrilateral faces can be turned on separately. For SOLID elements that have both triangular and quadrilateral faces both checks must be turned on to test all faces (otherwise only the triangular or quadrilateral faces will be tested, depending on which check is turned on and which is turned off).
