###  Mesh Morphing

Morph , under Mesh Tools, allows you to deform the shape of a mesh contained within a morph box. Morph boxes are a PRIMER only entity, made from morph points (also a PRIMER only entity - see [Appendix R](r-morphing-keyword-format.md#morphing_keyword_format) for the format of these keywords), which only affect nodes selected at the time of box creation. Nodal coordinates are recalculated based on their relative positions within a morph box, hence moving a/multiple morph points will alter the nodal coordinates.
 
The Morph panel is made up of four tabs: Create, Morph, Utilities, and Variables.
