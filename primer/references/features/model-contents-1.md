##  Model Contents

There are two ways of listing the contents of a model:

| [MODEL](model-contents.md#381MODELLIST) [&gt; CONTENTS](model-contents.md#381MODELLIST) | General listing of all model contents to screen and/or dialogue area and/or file |
| --- | --- |
| [UTILITIES](write-summary-file.md#382UTILITIESWRITESUMMARYFILE) [&gt; WRITE\_SUMMARY\_FILE](write-summary-file.md#382UTILITIESWRITESUMMARYFILE) | More specialised list of Part, Material, Contact and Element properties |
