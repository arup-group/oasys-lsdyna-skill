### PopupWindows

PopupWindows can be linked to widgets by setting the popupWindow property.

![](../Storage/primer-22-1/project-common-topics/js_gui_builder/popupwindow1.png)

![](../Storage/primer-22-1/project-common-topics/js_gui_builder/popupwindow2.png)

To remove a PopupWindow linked to a widget, set the popupWindow to &lt;no popup&gt;.
