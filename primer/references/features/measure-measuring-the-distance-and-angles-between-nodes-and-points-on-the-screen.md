##  MEASURE Measuring the Distance and Angles between Nodes and Points on the Screen

| ![](../Storage/primer-22-1/primer_links/sect_6/measure/fig_tools_measure.png) | The MEASURE command is invoked from the **Tools** panel at the top of the screen or from the shortcut key M. The following table gives a summary of the available options:<br> <br><br>| [**Point to Point**](point-to-point.md#651) | The (x,y) distance between two screen points P1 and P2. |<br>| --- | --- |<br>| [**Point angle**   ****](point-angle.md#652) | The angle between vectors P1P2 and P1P3. |<br>| [**Node to Node**](node-angle.md#654) | The (x,y,z) distance between N1 and N2, where N1 and N2 are either nodes or geometry points. |<br>| [**Node angle**](node-angle.md#654) | The angle between vectors N1N2 and N1N3, where N1, N2, N3 are either nodes or geometry points. |<br>| [**Nodal coord**](nodal-coordinate.md#655) | The coordinate of node/geometry point N1. |<br>| [**Curve length**](curve-length.md#656) | The length of the selected geometry curve. |<br>| [**Node to Part**](node-to-part.md#657) | The (x,y,z) distance between N and P, where N is either node or geometry point and P a part. |<br>| [**Part to Part**](part-to-part.md#658) | The (x,y,z) distance between P1 and P2, where P1 and P2 are both parts. |<br>| [**Node Curve length**](node-curve-length.md#659) | Cumulative length for node selections. |<br>| [**Distance Plot**](distance-plot.md#distance_plot) | The distance between two groups of parts. |<br>| [**Node to Plane**](node-to-plane.md#n2pl) | The distance from N4 to plane (N1N2N3). |<br>| [Node to Line](node-to-line.md) | The shortest distance from N3 to the line defined by nodes N1 and N2. | |

| ![](../Storage/primer-22-1/primer_links/sect_6/measure/fig_6_5_0b.gif) Screen "points" are simply transient 2d locations on the display picked with the cursor. They do not have any structural significance and are not part of any model. Distances and angles computed from them are in the 2d screen (x,y) space system. <br>In this example the user has selected two points, labelled P1 and P2 on the screen, and the 2D projected vector between them is to be computed.<br> <br>The reported distance, and its orientation with respect to the model, will be a function of the current transformation matrix.<br> <br>Therefore point-based measurement should be used when projected distances are required. For true 3D model space measurement it is better to use nodes. |
