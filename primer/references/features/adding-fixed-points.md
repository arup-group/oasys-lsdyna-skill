#####  Adding "Fixed" Points

| Fixed intermediate points effectively form a fixed end to the adjacent sections, and this would be unusual in the case of a normal lap and diagonal 3 point belt.<br>
<br>However, as this example shows, were you to create a full four or five point harness you would have to do this in two or three separate belt fitting operations, each meeting at a common point at the centre of the abdomen.<br>
