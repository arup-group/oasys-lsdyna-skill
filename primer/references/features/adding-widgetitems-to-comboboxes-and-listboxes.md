### Adding widgetitems to comboboxes, listboxes and radiobuttons

To add WidgetItems to a Combobox, Listbox or Radiobutton, right-click on it and select Edit WidgetItems. This will update the design window where you can add WidgetItems by pressing the Add New WidgetItem button.

![](../Storage/primer-22-1/project-common-topics/js_gui_builder/widgetitem1.png)

![](../Storage/primer-22-1/project-common-topics/js_gui_builder/widgetitem2.png)

The appearance of the current WidgetItem can be modified in the same way as Widgets by clicking on the WidgetItem and updating its properties.
To delete a WidgetItem, click on the - on the right hand side.
Once you have finished, press Apply to return to the normal design window.

![](../Storage/primer-22-1/project-common-topics/js_gui_builder/widgetitem3.png)
