###  Thick Cut

### 

![](../Storage/primer-22-1/thick-cut-2022-08-03.png)Alternative "thick" plane display mode. In this mode the plane is extruded in the local Z axis by +/- Thickness/2.0 either side of its "thin" position, effectively forming two planes with solid structure in between.

| This example shows the model above rendered in this way. <br>Note that nothing is displayed outside the extruded +ve and -ve planes, (and the Positive and Negative action options are inoperative). | ![](../Storage/primer-22-1/primer_links/sect_6/cutsect/demo_8.jpg) |
