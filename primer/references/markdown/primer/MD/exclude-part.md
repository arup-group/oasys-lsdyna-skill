### Exclude

By default PRIMER will apply the cutting plane to all the Parts/Part Sets in the model.![](../Storage/primer-22-1/exclude-part-2022-08-01.png)

This option can be used to exclude individual Part/Part Sets from the cutting plane so they can be seen on either side of the plane regardless of the +ve and -ve action settings.

Here is an example showing some structure cut by a section.

![](../Storage/primer-22-1/exclude-part-2022-08-01-1.png)

By selecting some parts to be excluded from the cut section they can be made visible.![](../Storage/primer-22-1/exclude-part-2022-08-01-2.png)
