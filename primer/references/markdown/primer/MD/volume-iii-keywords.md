###  Volume III Keywords

By default PRIMER shows the keywords from Volumes I and II of the keyword manual (as shown in the above image). You can toggle between viewing the Volumes I & II keywords and the Volume III keywords by pressing the buttons above the list of keywords.

| The volume III keywords are grouped together by solver. For example, there are keywords \*CESE\_CONTROL , \*EM\_CONTROL and \*ICFD\_CONTROL . <br>When showing Volume III keywords PRIMER will show the keywords for a single "main" keyword category. For example, the image on the right shows the keywords for \*CESE ( \*CESE\_BOUNDARY , \*CESE\_CONTROL , \*CESE\_EOS etc). | ![](../Storage/primer-22-1/primer_links/sect_5/5a/volIII_cese.png) |
| --- | --- |

| To show a different Volume III keyword press the appropriate keyword button. For example if ICFD is pressed PRIMER then shows the keywords for \*ICFD ( \*ICFD\_BOUNDARY , \*ICFD\_CONTROL , \*ICFD\_DATABASE etc).<br> <br>The other keywords are available at any time by using the popups. For example the \*EM keywords are still available in the image on the right by right clicking on EM . | ![](../Storage/primer-22-1/primer_links/sect_5/5a/volIII_icfd.png) |
| --- | --- |

| Inside MESH\_SURFACE\_ELEMENT dock menu, specialized CONVERT Button is provided to Convert ELEMENT\_SHELL to MESH\_SURFACE\_ELEMENT and vice-versa.<br> <br>The Function also converts the referenced PARTs and NODEs to MESH\_PARTs and MESH\_NODEs and vice-versa.<br> <br>The converted Elements/Mesh Elements and referenced Parts/Mesh Parts and Nodes/Mesh Nodes can be deleted via the Delete box. | ![Convert Mesh Element](../Storage/primer-22-1/primer_links/sect_5/5a/convert_element.png) |
