####  Setting the Fold Direction

| As well as setting the fold angle, the direction in which the fold is done needs to be set. In the adjacent figure the fold angle is set to 45 (you can tell this because of the angle of the yellow line). The graphic shown for the direction of the fold is also drawn at the 45 angle but there are two possible ways to do this fold<br> <br>The upper right part of the airbag can be folded onto the lower left part of the airbag (Forward). | ![](../Storage/primer-22-1/primer_links/sect_6/airbag/angle_direction.gif) |
| --- | --- |

The lower left part of the airbag can be folded onto the upper right part of the airbag (Reverse).

You can select the fold direction by choosing either Forward or Reverse. The graphic on the Direction button will change to indicate your selection.
