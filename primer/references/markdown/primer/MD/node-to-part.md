###  Node to Part

Node to part measurement computes the vector between nodes/geometry points N and part P, and reports it as model space (x,y,z) and magnitude components.

Nodes/parts may either be screen-picked, or have their label typed in, or use the standard popup options. Nodes and parts need not be in the same model.
