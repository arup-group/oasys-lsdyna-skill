### Setting the properties of widgets

The properties of a widget can be modified in the properties window, e.g. change the category to CATEGORY\_APPLY.

![](../Storage/primer-22-1/project-common-topics/js_gui_builder/properties1.png)

The appearance of the widget will update in the design window. If multiple widgets are selected the property will be applied to all the selected widgets.

![](../Storage/primer-22-1/project-common-topics/js_gui_builder/properties2.png)
