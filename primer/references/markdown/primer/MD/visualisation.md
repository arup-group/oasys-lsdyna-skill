\*IGA keywords supported with visualisation can be found in the [Entities panel](controlling-entity-visibility-and-labelling.md).

![](../Storage/primer-22-1/visualisation/iga_entities_panel.png)

Visualisation of \*IGA\_SHELLs is supported for both those that refer to \*IGA\_FACE\_XYZs and those that refer to \*IGA\_FACE\_XYZ\_BASIS\_TRANSFORMs. However, visualisation of \*IGA\_SOLIDs is only supported for those referring to \*IGA\_VOLUME\_XYZs.

Rendering of \*IGA keywords is affected by the settings related to Geometry on the 'Display Options' panel. Details of these settings can be found [here](display-options-controlling-plot-parameters.md#Geometry_Options).
