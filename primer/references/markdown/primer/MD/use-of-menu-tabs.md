###  Use of Menu Tabs

Docked menus appear in the Current Menu Panel, and hence do not obscure the graphics area. Any number of such menus can be present concurrently; these are positioned on top of one another. Each menu has a corresponding tab in the Menu Tabs area, and can be bought to the fore by clicking on its tab.

The [Model Menu](model-manipulation.md) and [Part Tree](part-tree.md) are always present in the tab list; other menus are invoked by the user. When there are more than eight menus open, the user can scroll through the open tabs using the left and right scroll tab buttons.
