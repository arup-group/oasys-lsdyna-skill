###  A Brief Description of How Modern Graphics Hardware Works

All modern workstations and PCs separate the main processor, the graphics card and the display. At its simplest:

| **The Main Processor**<br> <br>(CPU, etc) | *Sends graphics requests to* | **The Graphics Card**<br> <br>Which has its own memory and, in the case of better cards, considerable "intelligence" for 3D graphics (lighting, shading, hidden surface removal, etc) | *Sends video output to* | **The Display**<br> <br>**Cathode ray tube, LCD, plasma, projector, etc** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
