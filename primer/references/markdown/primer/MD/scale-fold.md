####  Scale Fold

 ![](../Storage/primer-22-1/primer_links/sect_6/airbag/scale_fold_button.gif) 

| The scale fold is used for shrinking or enlarging parts of the origami.<br> <br>The scaling is done in the local co-ordinate system of the fold, NOT the global co-ordinate system.<br> <br>To help you visualise the local co-ordinate system the axis triad is drawn on the airbag.<br> <br>The nodes/elements that you want to scale are selected in the normal way using sets and layers as required.<br> <br>You can give separate scale factors for the X, Y and Z axes of the fold. | ![](../Storage/primer-22-1/primer_links/sect_6/airbag/scale_fold.gif) |
