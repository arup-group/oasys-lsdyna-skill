####  Saving into the Group

Once you have chosen the to and from fields for the parts that you want to add the APPLY button becomes active. You can save them into the group by pressing the APPLY button.

![](../Storage/primer-22-1/primer_links/sect_6/groups/groups_7g.gif)

After the selection is saved to the group the screen refreshes back to the main screen (shown above). The feedback button (at the top of the image) changes to show that 1selection of PARTs by range has been added in this example. Additionally this is shown on the PART entity button. In this way you can easily see what entity types are present in the group.
