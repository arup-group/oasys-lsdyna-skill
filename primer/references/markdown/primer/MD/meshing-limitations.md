###  Meshing Limitations

The hole [creation](create-hole-in-mesh.md#createHole), [removal](remove-hole-from-mesh.md#removeHole) and [area](remesh-area.md#remeshArea) mesh features in PRIMER were new for version 10.0 and the [cobweb mesh](cobweb-mesh.md#cobwebmesh) was added in version 11.0 . They were an initial attempt to add some meshing capabilities to PRIMER and were quite limited.

In version 13.0 the remesh area feature was completely rewritten and multiple hole removal was added.

The tetrahedral and basic geometry meshing were added in version 14.0

It is expected that the mesh quality and meshing functionality will improve over time with subsequent releases.
