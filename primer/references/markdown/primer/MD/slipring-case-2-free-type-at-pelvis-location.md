#####  Slipring Case 2: Free Type at Pelvis Location

At the pelvis the slipring lies between two sections of belt that are both curved and thus free to move during fitting. Its orientation is unknown, and cannot be deduced from its location or the belt geometry, so instead of attempting to control the twist of the belt path it simply adopts the average twist angle and orientation of the two sections attached to it.
