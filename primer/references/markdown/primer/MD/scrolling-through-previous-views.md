###  Scrolling Through Previous Views

The ![](../Storage/primer-22-1/primer_links/sect_9/images/view_back.gif)and ![](../Storage/primer-22-1/primer_links/sect_9/images/view_forward.gif)buttons allow scrolling through previous views. Up to 100 previous views are stored automatically, and you can cycle backwards or forwards through these at will. The view storage "wraps round" at its ends, so going forwards past view #100 takes you to view #1 again.
