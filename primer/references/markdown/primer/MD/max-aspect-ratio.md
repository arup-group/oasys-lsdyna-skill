####  Max Aspect Ratio

For each face of an element, the length of each edge is calculated. The ratio of the lengths of each adjacent edge is then calculated. The largest (in the element) of these ratios is then compared with the specified maximum aspect ratio criteria. When calculating the ratios, the numerator is always the largest of the two lengths.
