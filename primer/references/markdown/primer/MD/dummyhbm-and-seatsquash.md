##  DUMMY/HBM AND SEATSQUASH

From v15.0 onwards PRIMER can create models to position a dummy/HBM and squash the seat foam in a single Ansys LS-DYNA analysis. It does this by creating cables to pull the dummy/HBM into position and means that interactions between the various parts are taken into account.
