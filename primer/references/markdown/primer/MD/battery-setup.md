The Battery Setup tool can be used to easily setup a pouch battery cell structure and combine it with the required \*EM\_RANDLES keywords to model Randles circuits within the battery cell.

The Ansys LS-DYNA Randles circuit model simulates the internal electrochemical reaction of a battery cell which could be used to analyse its thermal response during the charge or discharge cycles as well as predict internal short circuits during abusive scenarios.
