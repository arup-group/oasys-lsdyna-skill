### Label Background
Labels have a background by default to increase the legibility of the text.

![](../Storage/primer-22-1/label-background/label-background-2024-03-20-2.png)
The background can be turned off in Display Options &gt; Text & Labels &gt; Label Background or by setting a preference primer\*label\_background.

![](../Storage/primer-22-1/label-background/label-background-2024-03-20-1.png)
