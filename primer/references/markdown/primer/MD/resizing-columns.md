#### Resizing Columns

Columns can be dragged to resize by clicking and dragging the right edge of the respective column's header field.
 
Individual columns can also be resized to fit the longest entry by double-clicking on the right edge of that column's header field.
 
![](../Storage/primer-22-1/primer_links/sect_5/5a/kwe_13.png)
 Auto fit cols is used to resize all the columns widths to fit their longest entry.![](../Storage/primer-22-1/primer_links/sect_5/5a/kwe_14.png)
Reset cols is used to reset all the columns resizing and return them to their default widths.
